### 🔭 Featured work

**ML for Lung Cancer Prediction**: *Hospital Clínic / IDIBAPS* (private, clinical data)
End-to-end pipeline on two harmonized clinical cohorts (MIRROR & EXPOSOME) to predict, from a patient's first appointment:
- tumor **histology**, **cancer stage**, and **molecular driver status** (multi-class, ordinal and multi-label classifiers)
- **overall and progression-free survival** (Cox models, risk scores, Kaplan–Meier stratification)
- **interpretability**: SHAP-based analysis, including sex-conditional feature attention
- **deployment**: models tracked with MLflow and served through a **FastAPI** backend and a **Next.js** frontend

**[Algorithmic Approaches to Bin Packing](https://github.com/blaihernandez/Different-Algorithmic-Approaches-to-the-Bin-Packing-Porblem
)**: *C++*
Exhaustive search, greedy heuristics and a metaheuristic compared on the same bin packing problem.
